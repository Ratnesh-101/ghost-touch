# Lane lab-explore4: Explore toolbar, title, canvas fill (Explore and Correct), layout shift, alive state tidy-up

The supervisor reviewed real browser screenshots (390x844 and 1440x900) of the merged app. Read `docs/briefs/lab-spec.md`, `docs/reports/audit-layout.md` (section 5 on canvas sizing) and `docs/briefs/agy-lab-explore.md` first.

## Problems seen
1. EXPLORE still shows the OLD language and speech-speed selectors across the top (about 250 px of a 844 px screen). Capture already replaced them with ONE settings button and the `LabSettings` sheet. Do the same in Explore.
2. EXPLORE title truncates with an ellipsis ("Right Triangl…") on phones. It must wrap to two lines instead.
3. EXPLORE and CORRECT: the `<canvas>` fills only part of its frame. On a phone the Explore frame is about 440 px tall but the canvas is a wide strip inside it (a lighter rectangle on a black container); in Correct the diagram is drawn in a 177 px square inside a frame about 440 px tall, so it is small and hard to edit. The canvas must FILL its frame (the scene stays letterboxed and centred INSIDE the canvas by the existing viewport logic) and the canvas background must equal the container background (no lighter rectangle on black).
4. CORRECT: the canvas jumps about 47 px up after the first drag because the status line changes height. Reserve the status area's height (a `min-height` that fits two lines, also in Hindi and Gujarati) so nothing moves.
5. The alive moment added to `Explore.tsx` keeps its "already played" state in module-level mutable variables mutated inside a `useState` initializer. That is fragile (React StrictMode runs initializers twice, so in development the spark may not play) and untestable. Replace it with a small pure module `frontend/src/flow/alive.ts` (a registry with functions like `shouldPlayAlive(scene)`, `markAlivePlayed(scene)`, `markReturningFromCorrect()` and a reset for tests) and read it from a `useEffect`/`useRef`, with NO side effects inside render or state initializers. Behaviour stays the same: it plays once per freshly loaded scene, not when coming back from Correct or Ask.
