Do not touch the tuned haptic or audio values.
Scope (the audit's order):
A11Y-01: the Explore canvas must expose a spoken accessible name (role and aria-label or aria-labelledby tied to the scene title and the double-tap-and-hold hint), localised en/hi/gu; check the current markup first.
A11Y-04: when the language changes (and on first load), set `document.documentElement.lang` to the matching BCP-47 tag (en, hi, gu) so TalkBack uses the right voice; test it.
A11Y-02: in Settings, changing a language or speech rate must produce exactly ONE spoken confirmation (the speech driver cancels the previous utterance; two back-to-back `speak` calls make the first inaudible): join into one utterance or remove the duplicate.
A11Y-03: the Honesty/Verified banner (`VerifiedBanner.tsx`): its interactive part must be a real button with at least 48x48 px target and an accessible name; keep the "unverified/not cryptographic proof" meaning; never add the word "verified" to any new string.
Others (focus order, contrast ratios from the tokens, `prefers-reduced-motion`): do only what the audit CONFIRMS and is clearly safe; list the rest.
Every fix needs a regression test that fails without it. Keep every existing test green; never weaken an assertion.
