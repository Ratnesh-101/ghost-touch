# Lane K: User-test kit (docs only)
## Goal
A ready-to-use kit for a short, respectful test session with blind and low-vision students at a school for the blind (about 20 minutes, 2 to 4 students and a teacher).
## Allowed files
docs/user-test/** (new files and edits to plan.md), docs/reports/agy-usertest-report.md
## Forbidden
everything else. NEVER include any phone number, email address, street address or personal name of any person or organisation contact. Use placeholders like [CONTACT] and [DATE].
## Read first
docs/user-test/plan.md, docs/WIN_PLAN.md section on the school session, .kiro/steering/product.md
## Do
1. docs/user-test/consent-script.md: a spoken consent script (plain language, read aloud, includes guardian consent for under-18s, voluntary participation, right to stop, what is recorded: notes and hands-and-phone photos only, no faces, how data is used and deleted). Written to be READ ALOUD to blind participants.
2. docs/user-test/task-sheet.md: the tasks in order (find the corners of the triangle, the right angle, count sine peaks, tallest bar, one voice question), what the facilitator says word for word, what to observe, time boxes, and what to do if the phone or network fails (offline samples, Raw Mode).
3. docs/user-test/results-template.md: a table to record per participant: task, completed yes/no, time, errors, quotes, TalkBack on/off, and a short post-session questionnaire read aloud (5 questions, 1-5 scale spoken, plus open comments). Include a short summary section for the STEMergent write-up.
4. docs/user-test/outreach-email-draft.md: a short, polite draft request to a school for the blind with [CONTACT], [DATE] placeholders; mention the 30-second prototype video attachment; no claims we cannot back.
5. docs/user-test/facilitator-checklist.md: pre-session phone checklist (HTTPS URL loaded, volume, vibration enabled, TalkBack on/off tested, offline samples loaded, spare phone), and teardown.
## Done
Files exist, are consistent with plan.md, contain no real contact details. Report lists files touched. You only need to edit docs.
