## Real defects (likely files)

1. **D1, medium: vertices on the canvas edge after an AI scene.** In the uploaded-triangle result, C and B sit at x = 0 and x = 1. Their dots are cut in half by the canvas border and the "B" label is not drawn (`shots/19-upload-result.png`). The prompt normalizes coordinates to the diagram's bounding box, and the viewport has no inset, so any AI scene puts its extreme points exactly on the edge. That is hard to find by finger. Likely files: `frontend/src/core/geometry.ts` (letterbox viewport, no margin) or the normalization in `backend/app/vision/prompts.py` / `validate.py`. The model also moved A to x ≈ 0.25, so the right angle at C was lost, which is why "facts and drawing disagree on BC" was spoken. That warning is correct behaviour; the imprecise coordinates are a known risk (teacher correction is the fix).
2. **D2, low: `host.build.next` is never spoken.** Build speaks `build.prompt` ("Set x and y, then press Pin.") instead of the defined "Choose x and y on the sliders, then press Pin." Likely files: `frontend/src/screens/Build.tsx` and `src/i18n/index.ts:660`. Pick one string and delete the other.
3. **D3, low: wording mismatch in Settings.** Opening says "Settings opened" but closing says "Lab settings closed", and that line also shows in the visible Capture status card (hi: "लैब सेटिंग्स बंद"). Files: `frontend/src/components/LabSettings.tsx:253,286` and `lab.settings.closed` in `src/i18n/index.ts` (en, hi, gu).
4. **D4, info: a health GET on every Start load.** This is a deliberate warm-up, but on the free tier every page load wakes Render, and the call is not deduplicated across reloads (3 per run in this test). Consider a once-per-session flag.

## Could not verify

- That a real motor buzzes, how voices sound, TalkBack focus order and gestures: Chromium only records the calls.
- Hindi and Gujarati voices: headless Chromium has none. The app correctly said "I don't have a Hindi voice on this phone…" in English and then spoke the Hindi text. Whether a phone voice pronounces it well is not checked.
- Voice Ask (microphone): recognition was disabled, so only the typed fallback was tested.
- The 1.5 s pulse tone while an upload is waiting: frequencies were not sampled during the upload.
- Camera capture (`capture="environment"`): only the "Choose image" file input was used.
- Two-finger swipe-down and two-finger long-press: not simulated.
- The PWA install prompt, and offline on a cold first visit (only tested after one online load).
- Real 4G or a real budget phone CPU: the timings are CDP emulation on a desktop CPU.
