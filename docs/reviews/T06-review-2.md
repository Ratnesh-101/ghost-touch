| 1 | Medium | src/components/StatusText.tsx:20-23 | When `canSpeak` is true the status line is `aria-hidden`. `canSpeak` only means "speechSynthesis exists", not "speech was heard". A TalkBack user who missed or was interrupted on a message (including every error, and the arrival prompt) cannot swipe to it and re-read it, so the only text copy is hidden. TalkBack also announces the newly focused control (`takeButton.focus()` on mount and after errors, `back.focus()` in ExploreStub), which can overlap the app's own speech. A static, non-live paragraph is NOT auto-announced, so hiding it is not needed to avoid double reading. | Do not use `aria-hidden` when `spoken`. Render a plain `<p>` (no `aria-live`, no `role=status`) so it is swipe-reachable but silent until visited. Keep `role=status` only for the `!canSpeak` case. Update the screens.render test that asserts `aria-hidden`. Then confirm on the phone that TalkBack does not read it by itself. |
