We are building a free web-based tool designed to help blind and low-vision students access STEM diagrams (geometry shapes, math graphs, and science charts) directly on standard Android smartphones.

### How It Works
Traditional tactile diagrams require specialized embossers or swell paper that take days or weeks to prepare. Ghost Touch uses the phone’s built-in vibration motor and synthesized audio to make diagrams touch-accessible on demand:
- Tracing a line creates continuous haptic feedback.
- Touching a corner or vertex produces a distinct pulse and speaks the label (e.g., "Point A", "Right angle").
- Following a math curve plays a rising or falling tone that indicates height.
- Crucially, it is built to work alongside Google Android’s **TalkBack** screen reader.

We have attached a short 30-second video demonstrating how the touch interaction works on a standard phone screen.
