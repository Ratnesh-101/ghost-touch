# Spoken Consent & Assent Script

> **Notice for Facilitator:**  
> This script is written to be **read aloud** to blind and low-vision participants, their teachers, and parents/guardians.  
> Text marked **[SPOKEN]** should be read aloud word-for-word in a calm, clear, unhurried voice.  
> Text marked **[FACILITATOR NOTE]** indicates stage directions, pause points, or procedural instructions.  
> Never pressure anyone to participate. Respect any hesitation immediately.

---

## 1. Information & Student Assent (Read Aloud to the Student)

**[FACILITATOR NOTE: Introduce yourself and sit at table level with the student. Ensure ambient noise is low.]**

**[SPOKEN]**  
"Hello [STUDENT_NAME]. My name is [FACILITATOR_NAME]. Thank you so much for meeting with us today.

We are working on a prototype tool called **Ghost Touch**. It is designed to run on a normal Android phone. The idea is to help blind and low-vision students feel math and science diagrams using vibration and sound. When you move your finger across the screen, the phone buzzes on lines, pulses at corners, speaks labels, and plays tones that go up and down with graph curves.

Today, we want to learn from you whether this actually works and what we need to improve. 

Here are the most important things to know before we begin:

1. **You are not being tested.** The phone app is being tested. If something is confusing, difficult, or doesn't work, that is the app's fault, never yours.
2. **Participation is completely voluntary.** You can take a break at any moment, you can skip any task, and you can stop whenever you want. You do not have to give any reason, and nobody will be upset.
3. **Your comfort and safety come first.** The phone uses sound and vibration. If any vibration tickles, startles you, feels uncomfortable, or if the sound is too loud, please tell me immediately and we will stop or adjust it right away.
4. **What we write down:** I will be taking notes on paper or a computer about how the app responds and how many seconds things take. We will not record your name in our notes—we will only use a code like P1 or P2.
5. **Photos or video are optional:** We would love to take a short photo or video clip of your **hands on the phone** while you explore a shape, to show other engineers how the touch interaction works. We will **never** record your face. If you or your guardian prefer that we take **no photos or videos at all**, that is 100% fine. You can still do the entire test with just written notes.
6. **Data privacy:** Any notes or photos collected are used solely for evaluating this project for the student hackathon. They will never be sold, and no personal contact information will ever be stored in our files or shared publicly.

Do you have any questions for me about what we are doing?"

**[FACILITATOR NOTE: Pause and answer any questions thoroughly.]**

**[SPOKEN]**  
"Would you like to try the prototype with us today?"

**[FACILITATOR NOTE: Await clear verbal assent: 'Yes'. If the student says no or hesitates, thank them warmly and stop.]**

## 2. Parent / Guardian / Teacher Consent (Under 18)

**[FACILITATOR NOTE: Required for any participant under 18 years of age. Read aloud or provide in preferred accessible format to the parent, guardian, or authorized institutional representative.]**

**[SPOKEN]**  
"Hello [GUARDIAN_NAME]. We are conducting a 20-minute usability evaluation of Ghost Touch, an open-source assistive prototype that renders STEM diagrams as haptic vibrations, synthesized speech, and audio tones on Android smartphones.

- **Purpose:** To understand how blind students explore tactile geometry and graphs using Android touch gestures with TalkBack enabled.
- **Duration:** Approximately 15 to 20 minutes.
- **Activities:** The student will be taught a simple Android touch gesture (double-tap and hold, then drag), explore 3 to 4 sample diagrams (a triangle, a sine curve, a bar chart), and ask one voice question.
- **Risks & Discomforts:** Minimal. The device produces standard smartphone vibration and audio through speakers or headphones. The session will pause or end immediately if the student experiences any discomfort, sensory overload, or fatigue.
- **Voluntary:** Participation is entirely voluntary. The student or guardian may withdraw consent at any time without penalty.
- **Privacy & Anonymity:** All records are pseudonymized (e.g., Participant P1). No names, phone numbers, or addresses are included in public research repositories or reports.
- **Media Options:** You may choose whether we may record hands-and-phone video/photography, or opt for written notes only. Faces are strictly excluded from all recordings."
